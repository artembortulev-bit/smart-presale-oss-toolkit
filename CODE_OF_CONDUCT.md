# Code Of Conduct

## Expected Behavior

- Be direct, respectful, and specific.
- Discuss technical trade-offs with evidence.
- Keep reviews focused on behavior, architecture, security, and maintainability.
- Respect privacy boundaries around customer data, private assets, and local environments.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, personal attacks, or discriminatory language.
- Publishing private data, secrets, or local credentials.
- Pressuring contributors to disclose private customer or commercial information.
- Repeatedly derailing technical discussion.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove content, close threads, or restrict participation when behavior harms the project or violates privacy/security rules.

For sensitive concerns, contact the maintainer privately when a private channel is available.
